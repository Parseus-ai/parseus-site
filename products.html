Maestro AI

Get your hours back, without sacrificing the quality of what you send.

AI decodes. You decide. We deliver.

Maestro AI helps job seekers discover roles, build tailored applications and track everything in one place, while they keep control of what represents them and every decision that matters. It never applies for you.

Instead of one model and one prompt, Maestro uses specialized agents, each responsible for one part of the workflow. You decide what each agent knows, how it behaves, and which AI model runs it. It’s free, runs on your own machine, and you connect your own AI account.

Read the documentation (opens in a new tab) Download on GitHub (opens in a new tab)

What it does

  • Finds roles at your target companies
  • Scores every job for fit against your background
  • Tailors your resume and cover letter for the roles you choose
  • Critiques and fact-checks the output, then hands the final call to you
  • Tracks applications and versions over time
