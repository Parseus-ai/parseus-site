How each piece is written

  1. What does it enable?What the underlying research really makes possible, and what it doesn’t.
  2. What does it require?The architecture, data and integration needed to implement it.
  3. What could go wrong?Practical constraints, failure modes and risks.
  4. Will it hold up?Reliability and performance once it’s running in operations.
  5. Is it worth it?Where the measurable business value is, and where it isn’t.
