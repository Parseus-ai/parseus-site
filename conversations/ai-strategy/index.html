AI Strategy Conversations

A complimentary, one-to-one conversation for professionals working through a specific AI challenge: whether an idea is technically sound, what it would take to implement, or how to move a pilot into production.

  • 20 minutes
  • Ten initial places
  • Online
  • By application
  • No cost

Go to the application ↓

Who it is for

Professionals and teams with a concrete AI question in enterprise AI, robotics, manufacturing, computer vision, architecture or implementation.

What to prepare

  • A short, general description of the challenge
  • What you would like to get from the conversation
  • Where the project stands today

Please don’t share confidential information. Leave out trade secrets, customer data and anything covered by a non-disclosure agreement.

How selection works

  1. Every application is read individually.
  2. We select the applications where a short conversation is most likely to help, and let every applicant know the outcome by email.
  3. Selected applicants receive a private link to book a time.
  4. After the conversation, we send a short follow-up.
