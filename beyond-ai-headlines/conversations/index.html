Apply for a 20-minute AI strategy conversation

A focused, one-to-one conversation about a specific AI challenge: whether an idea is technically sound, what it would take to implement, or how to move a pilot into production. There is no cost.

How it works

  1. Application. Tell us about your challenge using this form.
  2. Review. Every application is read individually.
  3. Selection. We choose the applications where a short conversation is most likely to help, and let every applicant know the outcome by email.
  4. Private scheduling. Selected applicants receive a private link to book a time.
  5. Conversation. Twenty focused minutes, online.
  6. Follow-up. A short note with any next steps.

Please don’t share confidential information. A general description of your challenge is enough. Don’t include trade secrets, customer data, or anything covered by a non-disclosure agreement.

Online applications open soon.

Until then, you can apply by email: write to info@parseus.ai with your name, role, organization, the challenge you’d like to discuss, what you hope to get from the conversation, and how far along the project is.

Your application

All fields are required. It takes about five minutes.

What are you trying to do, and what is getting in the way? Please keep it general and non-confidential.
For example: a second opinion on an approach, a sense of what implementation would involve, or how to measure success.
