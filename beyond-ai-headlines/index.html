Beyond AI Headlines: From Innovation to Real-World Impact

Headlines announce what AI can do. Organizations need to know what it will take to make it work for them: the architecture, the operations, the risks and the value. Beyond AI Headlines brings that conversation into the open, through independent insights, complimentary one-to-one strategy conversations and monthly roundtables.

A

Professional insights

Independent analysisFirst pieces in preparation

Analysis that goes beyond announcing a new capability, and asks what it means for an organization that has to build, run and pay for it. Each piece looks at:

  • What the emerging research actually enables
  • Architecture and implementation requirements
  • Practical constraints and risks
  • Reliability and operational performance
  • Opportunities for measurable business value

Ten complimentary 20-minute AI strategy conversations

20 minutesOne-to-oneBy applicationTen places

A focused conversation about a specific AI challenge you’re facing: whether an idea is technically sound, what it would take to implement, or how to get a stalled pilot to production. There is no cost.

Because there are only ten places, we ask you to apply first. Every application is read and reviewed individually.

  1. ApplicationTell us about your challenge.
  2. ReviewWe read every application.
  3. SelectionSelected applicants are contacted by email.
  4. Private schedulingA private link to book a time.
  5. ConversationTwenty focused minutes.
  6. Follow-upA short note with next steps.

Please don’t include confidential or commercially sensitive information in your application. A general description of the challenge is enough.
